Company Policy - Remote Work and Security

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
All laptops must use full-disk encryption and auto-lock after 10 minutes.
Company data may only be stored in approved systems.
Sensitive customer data must not be copied to personal devices.
Employees must complete security awareness training once per quarter.
Reported incidents should be sent to security@example.local within 24 hours.
